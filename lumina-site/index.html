01 Intent

Start with what you mean, not with menus.

Talk to Lumina the way you'd brief a capable colleague. It works out the goal, the constraints and what “done” looks like — and when something is ambiguous, it asks instead of guessing.

  • Voice first, text any time. Speak naturally, or type when it's quiet.
  • Asks before it assumes. Unclear references get a question, not a guess.
  • You stay in charge. Interrupt, cancel, deny or correct at any point.

02 Memory

Context that keeps. Control that stays with you.

Lumina remembers what's worth keeping — preferences, decisions, unfinished work — not every word you've said. Each memory carries its source and how sure Lumina is, and any of it can be inspected, corrected or forgotten.

Memory inspector Example memories

In Lumina, memory lives in a local database on your PC. Recognizable credentials are filtered out before anything is stored, and nothing is shared with a cloud service without separate, explicit consent.

03 Action

Plan. Approve. Act. Verify.

Every request runs through the same disciplined loop. The model proposes; Lumina's runtime decides what is allowed — and anything with consequences waits for you.

  1. Understand Works out the goal, the constraints and what “done” looks like.
  2. Recall Brings in only the context that matters, with its source.
  3. Plan Breaks the work into steps, each with its risk and its check.
  4. Approve Changes, messages and anything irreversible wait for your explicit yes. Waiting for you
  5. Act Runs typed tools with timeouts and cancellation. Stop means stop.
  6. Verify Success is judged by evidence from your machine, not by the model's confidence.

04 Capabilities

One runtime. Many threads.

Every capability plugs into the same tool contract — typed inputs, declared permissions, timeouts, cancellation and evidence — so new skills extend Lumina without bypassing its rules. Select one to find its thread on the loom.
