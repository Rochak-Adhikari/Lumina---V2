## 3. Architecture & Interaction Design

### 3.1 Marker Distinction & Styling
To ensure immediate visual clarity against NASA Blue Marble imagery and atmosphere scattering:
- **Search Candidate Marker:** Cyan beacon (`0x38bdf8`) with cyan ring and pin stem (`0x93c5fd`).
- **Saved Atlas Waypoint Markers:** Warm amber/gold pin (`0xf59e0b`) with gold ring, subtle warm emissive head sphere (`0xb45309`), and stem (`0xfde68a`).
- **Selected Waypoint Marker:** Scaled up to 1.35x with bright cyan emissive glow (`0x38bdf8`, emissiveIntensity 1.4) and blue stem (`0x93c5fd`), making the active focus unmistakable.

### 3.2 3D Globe Raycast Click Interaction
To enable clicking waypoint markers directly on the rotating 3D globe without accidental triggers during camera orbiting:
- Pointer down and up events record Euclidean distance and duration:
  $$\Delta d = \sqrt{(x_{\text{up}} - x_{\text{down}})^2 + (y_{\text{up}} - y_{\text{down}})^2}$$
- A click is recognized only if $\Delta d < 6\text{ px}$ and $\Delta t < 500\text{ ms}$.
- `THREE.Raycaster` projects from the camera through normalized device coordinates (NDC) against all active waypoint child meshes.
- Clicking a waypoint marker on the globe automatically selects the waypoint in the Atlas controller, triggers camera fly-to, highlights the list card, and fetches live weather.

### 3.3 Stable Marker Identity Reconciliation
Rather than destroying and rebuilding all 3D meshes whenever waypoints update:
- Markers are managed in a `Map<string, MarkerObject>` keyed by waypoint ID.
- `setWaypoints(list)` performs incremental reconciliation:
  1. Identifies removed waypoints and explicitly calls `.dispose()` on their Three.js geometries and materials before removing from the Earth group.
  2. Retains existing markers whose coordinates have not changed.
  3. Repositions or creates new marker meshes only when coordinates change or new waypoints are added.

### 3.4 In-Place Delete Confirmation
To prevent accidental loss of saved places:
- Clicking "Delete" on any card does not trigger immediate deletion.
- The action row transitions inline into a confirmation banner: *"Delete this place?"* with *"Delete"* and *"Cancel"* buttons.
- Clicking *"Cancel"* restores normal card actions without calling the API.
- Clicking *"Delete"* invokes `DELETE /api/earth/waypoints/{id}` with `X-Lumina-Token`, clears selected state if the deleted place was active, and reloads the list.
